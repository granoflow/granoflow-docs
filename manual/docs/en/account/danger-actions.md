What dangerous actions have in common is that they have a wide impact, and some outcomes are not easy to reverse. Before acting, ask yourself: do I just want to switch accounts, clean up this device, or abandon this account?

## A Real‑World Scenario

If you mistake "logout" for "delete account", the consequences are completely different; if you mistake "clear local data" for a normal restart, you might lose unsynced content.

## Boundaries

::::caution[Back up first, then proceed]
For any operation that clears data, resets state, or deletes an account, first confirm backup and sync status. Do not repeatedly attempt multiple dangerous entries when you're uncertain.
