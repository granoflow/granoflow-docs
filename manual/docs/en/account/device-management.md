Device management is useful when you switch devices, lose a device, or want to review your sync status. It answers the question: which devices are associated with this account.

## A Real‑World Scenario

If you no longer use an old computer, first confirm that your new device has all the data you need, then handle the old device. This is safer than deleting while migrating.

## Boundaries

The device list is not a daily task list. You only need to focus on it when you are switching devices, troubleshooting sync, or reviewing account security.
