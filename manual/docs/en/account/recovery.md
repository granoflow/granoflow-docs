The goal of the recovery process is to regain access to your data and identity, not to create a new empty account.

## A Real Scenario

If you can’t see your old data on a new device, first check that the account is the same, then review sync, recovery keys, and the old device’s status.

## How to Decide What to Do Next

| Your situation | What to check first | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Don’t know where to start | Page title and main entry points | Select only the option relevant to your current goal |
| Results are not as expected after an action | Status, empty prompts, access logs, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot in order |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permission documentation | Stop first, confirm the scope, then continue |

## Boundaries

When recovering, troubleshoot in order: account, key, old device, sync status. Do not clear local data at the start.

## Next Step

After reading this section, return to your current task and choose only one minimal action: record an input, check a status, or open relevant settings to confirm.
