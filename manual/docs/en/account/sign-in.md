Signing in establishes your identity. You can use GranoFlow locally first, but once you involve multiple devices, subscription benefits, or restoration, you need to know which account you are currently signed in to.

## A Real‑World Scenario

Before switching devices, first confirm your account on the old device; after signing in on the new device, check that sync and data match your expectations.

## How to Decide Your Next Step

| Your situation | What to check first | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not sure where to start | The current page title and main entry points | Choose only the one item that matches your current goal |
| The result is not what you expected after an action | Status, empty prompts, access history, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot step by step |
| Worried about impacting your data | Backup, sync, account, or permissions documentation | Stop first, confirm the scope, then continue |

## Boundaries

After signing in, don’t just look at the button state – take a moment to check that your account, sync, and membership entry points all point to the same identity.
