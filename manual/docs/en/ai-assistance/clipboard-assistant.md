The clipboard is handy for temporarily handing a piece of text to AI for processing. Before using it, make sure it doesn't contain sensitive content you don't want to send or process.

## A real-world scenario

For example, if you copy a meeting agenda from an email, you can ask AI to extract tasks for you; but it's best to delete any personal information first.

## Boundaries

The clipboard entry point is very convenient, but it's also the easiest place to forget the source of content. Take a second look before sending.
