---
title: Why Confirmation Is Needed Before Changes
description: "Understand the confirmation boundary between AI suggestions and actual writes."
---

AI can suggest changes, but before actually writing to tasks, projects, or settings, your confirmation is required. This pause prevents organized suggestions from becoming incorrect data.

## A Real-World Scenario

If AI misinterprets a title as a project name, you can correct it before confirming. If it were written directly, you'd have to clean it up later.

## How to Decide the Next Step

| Your Situation | First Check | Next Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not sure where to start | Current page title and main entry points | Select only one item relevant to your current goal |
| Results are incorrect after an action | Status, empty prompts, access logs, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot step by step |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permission documentation | Stop first, confirm the scope, then continue |

## Boundary

Confirmation is not an unnecessary step. It lets AI handle the organizing while you keep the final say.
