The AI review can sort today’s task records into helpful threads, so you can quickly see what’s done, what’s stuck, and what’s worth remembering for next time.

## A Real-World Scenario

If you have several unfinished tasks today, AI can help group the reasons first. Then you decide which ones to keep, break down, or archive for tomorrow.

## How to Decide the Next Step

| Your Situation | What to Check First | Next Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not sure where to start | Current page title and main entry points | Pick only one item that relates to your current goal |
| Action didn’t give the expected result | Status, empty states, access history, or sync progress | Go back one level and investigate step by step |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permission info | Pause, confirm the scope, then continue |

## Boundaries

A review is not about judging yourself — it’s about organizing information for the next step.

## Next Step

After reading this section, return to the task you’re working on and choose only one smallest action to continue: log an input, check a status, or open related settings to confirm once.
