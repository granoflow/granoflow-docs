Redaction terms are suitable for handling names, numbers, or fixed phrases that you often don't want AI to see. It is not a complete security solution, but it can reduce obvious exposure.

## A Real Scenario

If customer names or project codes frequently appear in your tasks, you can add these terms to the redaction settings before using AI assistance.

After redaction, you still need to review the context. Even if terms are replaced, the context might still reveal meaning.
