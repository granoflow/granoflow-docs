Title parsing is useful for breaking a natural language sentence into task information that's easier to handle. It's not meant to plan your life for you—it simply organizes the input more clearly.

## A Real‑World Scenario

Enter “finish sorting project materials by Friday,” and AI may help identify the time, task action, and possible project association.

## How to Decide Your Next Step

| Situation | What to Check First | Next Step |

| The result is wrong after an action | Status, empty state, access history, or sync progress | Go back one level and check step by step |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permissions info | Stop first, confirm the scope, then proceed |

## Boundaries

Always review the result after parsing. Task titles, dates, projects, and reminders may need your adjustments.
