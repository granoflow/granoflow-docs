Account deletion is one of the few operations that requires you to slow down. It affects not only the login method, but also syncing, membership benefits, and cloud-related data.

## A Real Scenario

If you just want to sign out of the current device, you usually don’t need to delete your account. If you want to stop using your account, first back up your local data and confirm your membership and sync status.

| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permission documentation | Stop first, confirm the scope, then proceed |

## Boundaries

::::caution[Back Up Before Deleting]
Before deleting your account, confirm how your local data, synced data, and subscription benefits will be handled. Do not treat account deletion as a simple sign-out.
::::
