Backup answers the question “I need a portable copy.” It’s different from sync: sync keeps devices up to date, backup gives you a recoverable file when it matters.

## A Real‑World Scenario

Before reinstalling your OS, switching computers, or performing a high‑risk cleanup, create a local backup package and put the file somewhere you can find.

## Boundaries

::::caution[Keep your backup files safe]
A local backup package may contain your tasks, reviews, cards, and related data. Do not share it carelessly, and do not leave it only on the device you are about to wipe.
