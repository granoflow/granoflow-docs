A recovery key is more like a lock-opening tool, not an ordinary login password. Your account proves who you are, but the key determines whether you can open certain encrypted data.

## A Real-World Scenario

If you're preparing to switch devices, first confirm that your recovery key or old device is still accessible. Once the old device has been wiped, finding the key will be much more difficult.

## Boundaries

::::caution[Don't treat the key as a hint you can always recover]
If you lose the key, some encrypted data may not be accessible on a new device. Save it securely before switching devices or reinstalling.
::::
