---
title: What Does Local-First Mean
description: "Understand why GranoFlow stores your data locally first."
---

Local-first means your daily work shouldn't stop because of network conditions. Content you create and organize on your device is saved locally first.

## A Real-World Scenario

When organizing tasks on the subway, you should still be able to take notes even if the network is unstable. Syncing can happen later once the connection recovers.

## Boundaries

Local-first does not mean you can skip backups. Important data still requires attention to sync status and local backups.
