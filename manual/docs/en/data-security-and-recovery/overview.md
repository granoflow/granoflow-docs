Data security isn't something you solve with a single button. GranoFlow has at least four separate things: local storage, cloud sync, local backup, and attachment cleanup.

## A Real-world Scenario

When switching devices, look at sync and recovery; before reinstalling, look at local backup; when disk space is tight, look at attachments; when worried about account issues, start with recovery information.

## Three Entry Points for Three Types of Risk

![Data management](../../screenshots/en/route-data-management-settings-data-management-main.png)

The Data Management page is the place to handle backups, imports, card sets, and attachment cache. It's where you should go first before migrating, restoring, or cleaning up.

![Local backup progress](../../screenshots/en/route-local-backup-progress-settings-data-management-progress-main.png)

When the local backup progress appears, wait until the process finishes. Closing the app or cleaning files while a backup is still running will only make recovery harder later.

![Attachment cleanup](../../screenshots/en/route-clear-attachments-settings-clear-attachments-main.png)

Attachment cleanup only deals with space‑hogging files; it doesn't delete tasks themselves. Before cleaning, confirm whether you still need to open those images, PDFs, or media files in your records.

## How to Decide What to Do Next

| Your situation | Check first | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not sure where to start | Current page title and main entry points | Choose only the option that matches your current goal |
| Result after an action is wrong | Status, empty prompts, access logs, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot step by step |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permissions info | Stop first, confirm the scope, then continue |

## Boundaries

First, figure out which risk you need to guard against: lost device, device switch, sync interruption, or low local storage.
