Synchronization lets multiple devices see the same data, but it’s not the only form of backup. If sync stops, check the status first before deciding whether to proceed.

## A real‑world scenario

If you just wrote a lot of reviews on the desktop and they haven’t appeared on the phone yet, first check the sync progress – don’t immediately edit the same content back and forth on both devices.

## How to decide what to do next

| What’s happening | What to check first | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You’re not sure where to start | The current page title and main entry points | Pick just one item related to your current goal |
| After an action, the result isn’t what you expected | Status, empty state hints, access history, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot step by step |
| You’re worried about affecting data | Backups, sync, account, or permission notes | Stop first, confirm the scope, then continue |

## Boundaries

A practical rule for sync: wait until the current device finishes uploading, then check on the other device.
