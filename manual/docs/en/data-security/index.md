description: "Understand local-first use, sync, backup, and recovery boundaries."
docType: "Explanation"
readerGoal: "Know when to rely on the current device and when to check backup or sync."
---

Data guidance starts with one question: where is the current truth for your work? Granoflow is designed around current-device use, with sync, backup, and restore supporting safer continuity.

Local-first does not mean you never need backup. It also does not mean the cloud always has everything. For important work, check backup and sync before changing devices, deleting data, or resetting an account.

Do not clear local data as the first troubleshooting step. Preserve the current state, then diagnose.
