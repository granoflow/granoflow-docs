The advantage of the desktop isn’t just a bigger screen—it’s better suited for extended sessions of organizing, searching, comparing, and reviewing.

## A Real‑World Scenario

You can keep your task list on one side, put a document or browser on the other, and use Granoflow to record your next step.

## How to Decide Your Next Step

| Situation | First, look at | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not sure where to start | Current page title and main entry points | Select only one item related to your current goal |
| Result is wrong after an action | Status, empty state hints, access history, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot step by step |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permission notes | Stop first, confirm the scope, then proceed |

## Boundaries

The desktop is best for structured organizing, while the mobile app is better for quick capture. Choose the right tool for the task—there’s no need to make both ends do the same work.

## Next Steps

After reading this section, return to the task you were working on and pick just one small action to continue: record an input, check a status, or open the relevant settings to perform a confirmation.
