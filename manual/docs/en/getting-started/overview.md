Granoflow is for people who do not want useful work experience to disappear after a task is completed. It connects four layers: tasks, projects and milestones, reflection, and review cards.

Tasks hold concrete next actions. Projects and milestones give longer work a shape. Reflection keeps the decisions and lessons from completed work. Review cards turn reusable ideas into prompts you can practice later.

## Example

When you are preparing a paper, application, or product release, you need more than a to-do item. You collect ideas, turn them into tasks, place them inside a project, and later keep the decisions that helped you move forward.

Granoflow does not promise automatic productivity or personal outcomes. It helps you record, organize, review, and choose the next step.
