Granoflow is not mainly about how many boxes you can check today. The deeper question is whether your actions turn into clearer judgment. If you treat it as a plain to-do list, you miss how projects, domains, reflection, and cards fit together.

Think of it as a map for work: tasks are the next step, projects and domains explain why the step matters, reflection keeps what you learned, and cards help useful ideas return later.

## Where to start

Start from the sidebar, settings, or the current workflow entry point instead of relying on an old remembered path.

## How to use it

1. Confirm that the current page is the right entry point for the workflow.
2. Do the smallest useful action first, then observe how the page changes.
3. Move durable judgment back into tasks, projects, reflection, or cards.
