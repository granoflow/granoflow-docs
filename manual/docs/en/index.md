Granoflow keeps tasks, projects, reflection, and review cards in one workflow. You can capture work as tasks, place longer work inside projects and milestones, and keep useful experience as reflection notes or cards after the work is done.

This edition uses a new manual structure. It does not copy the deprecated manual, and it does not use old screenshots. Pages with approved screenshots include them; pages without approved screenshots stay text-based until the screenshot pipeline captures and reviews them.
