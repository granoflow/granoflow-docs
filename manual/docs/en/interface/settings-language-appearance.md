Appearance settings aren't just decoration. Language, theme, and font all affect whether you'll want to keep using this tool over the long term.

## A Real‑World Scenario

If you often organize tasks at night, a dark theme may be more comfortable. If you work in a multilingual environment, language settings influence how easily you understand manuals, buttons, and prompts.
