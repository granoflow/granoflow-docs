Value without action easily stays as ideas; action without direction easily becomes busywork.

## A Real-World Scenario

If you value professional growth, today's action might not be "become better" but "review a project communication and write an experience card."

## How to Determine the Next Step

| Your situation | First check | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Don't know where to start | Current page title and main entry points | Only choose one item related to your current goal |
| Operation yields wrong result | Status, empty prompts, access logs, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot in order |
| Worried about data impact | Backup, sync, account, or permission info | Stop first, confirm scope, then proceed |

## Boundaries

Translating a high-level direction into small actions is the most rewarding part of Granoflow to practice repeatedly.

## Next Steps

After reading this section, return to the task you are working on and pick just one minimal action to continue: record an input, check a status, or open relevant settings to perform a confirmation.
