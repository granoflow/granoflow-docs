A completed task can still contain useful experience. Reflection keeps what happened, what you learned, and what should happen next.

## Review Flow

1. Start from a task or project that moved forward.
2. Record the facts: what happened and what changed.
3. Record the judgment: what worked, what blocked you, and what is worth keeping.
4. Turn the next step back into a task when needed.
5. Turn reusable lessons into review cards when they may help again.

Reflection is not a self-evaluation score. It is a way to keep facts, decisions, and next steps visible.
