This entitlements guide is not a sales pitch — it helps you understand why a certain capability is currently unavailable.

## A Real-World Scenario

If a sync or advanced setting prompts you that membership is required, first check what type of capability it belongs to; if you have already purchased, verify that you are logged into the correct account.

## How to Decide Next Steps

| What you encounter | What to check first | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not sure where to start | Current page title and main entry | Pick only one item relevant to your current goal |
| Results are incorrect after an action | Status, empty prompts, access logs, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot in order |
| Worried about affecting data | Backups, sync, account, or permission documentation | Stop first, confirm the scope, then proceed |

## Boundaries

Entitlement status should be considered together with your account and platform purchase records. Do not rely on a single button to determine whether a purchase has taken effect.
