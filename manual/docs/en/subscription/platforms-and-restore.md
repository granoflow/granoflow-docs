Platform purchases have an easily overlooked detail: payment happens on the platform, while benefit display happens in your Granoflow account. The two sides need to match.

## A Real-World Scenario

If you made a purchase on the App Store or another platform but can't see the benefits on your current device, first log in to the same account, then proceed with Restore Purchases.

## Boundaries

Restore Purchases is meant to retrieve benefits you've already bought — it is not a new purchase. Before executing, confirm the account you're currently logged into.
