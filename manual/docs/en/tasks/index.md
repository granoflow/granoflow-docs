A task is the smallest action unit in Granoflow. It can enter the inbox while still rough, then become a clear step when you organize it.

Capture first, clarify later. This keeps small “I should remember this” items out of your head. They have a place to land, and you can decide date, project, reflection, or card later.

## Where to start

Start from the sidebar, settings, or the current workflow entry point instead of relying on an old remembered path.

## How to use it

1. Confirm that the current page is the right entry point for the workflow.
2. Do the smallest useful action first, then observe how the page changes.
3. Move durable judgment back into tasks, projects, reflection, or cards.
