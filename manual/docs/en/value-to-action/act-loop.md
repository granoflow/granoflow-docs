---
title: Choosing the Next Step When You’re Stuck
description: "Redirect your attention from overthinking to an actionable step."
---

When you face resistance, you don’t always need to get yourself into the right state first. A more practical approach is to acknowledge the resistance and then choose a small action you can still take.

## A Real‑Life Example

If you don’t feel like starting a paper, you don’t have to force yourself to write a whole section. Just open the document and list three sources you need to look up.

## How to Decide the Next Step

| Situation | Look at This First | Next Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Don’t know where to start | Current page title and main entry points | Select only one item related to your current goal |
| Operation gives wrong results | Status, empty states, access history, or sync progress | Go back one level and check step by step |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permission notes | Stop, confirm the scope, then continue |

## Limitations

GranoFlow can help you break the next step into a small action, but whether you actually start is still your choice.

## Next Steps

After reading this section, return to the task you were working on and choose just one minimal action to continue: log an input, check a status, or open a related setting to complete a confirmation.
