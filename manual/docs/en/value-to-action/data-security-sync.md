The more you use your action system, the more your data is worth protecting. Tasks, projects, reviews, and cards all carry your working threads.

## A Real‑World Scenario

Before changing computers, first check data management, sync progress, and adding a new device—don’t treat a migration as a normal login.

## Check These Three Things Before Changing Devices

![Data Management](../../screenshots/en/route-data-management-settings-data-management-main.png)

Data management is meant to be opened before a migration, backup, or cleanup. It answers the question: “Can I take or restore this data?”

![Sync Progress](../../screenshots/en/route-sync-progress-sync-main.png)

Sync progress tells you whether your data is still being transferred. Before switching devices, if this isn’t finished yet, wait until it completes.

Adding a new device to sync is the final step. Only after confirming the old device and account status should you let a new device join the same data link.

## How to Decide What to Do Next

| Situation You Encounter | What to Check First | Next Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Don’t know where to start | Current page title and main entry | Pick only the item related to your current goal |
| Result isn’t what you expected after an action | Status, empty hints, access records, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot in order |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permission details | Stop first, confirm scope, then continue |

## Boundaries

Protect your data before you optimize for convenience. This order matters especially when switching devices.
