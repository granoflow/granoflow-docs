The inbox catches input; the task list holds actions. When they get mixed together, you end up facing both "not yet figured out" and "do it now" at the same time.

## A Real-World Scenario

When you see "look into sync issues," put it in the inbox first; when you organize it, change it to "reproduce the mobile sync failure and record the error message."

## How to Decide the Next Step

| Situation | What to check first | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Don't know where to start | Current page title and main entry points | Select only one item related to your current goal |
| Result is wrong after an action | Status, empty state hints, access logs, or sync progress | Go back one level and troubleshoot step by step |
| Worried about affecting data | Backup, sync, account, or permission documentation | Stop first, confirm the scope, then continue |

## Edge Cases

A good task should tell you what to do next, not just remind you that something vague exists.

## Next Steps

After reading this section, go back to the task you were working on and pick one smallest action to continue: log an input, check a status, or open the relevant settings to confirm once.
